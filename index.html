HUMAN–COMPUTER INTERACTION · SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

Understanding people.
Building inclusive software.

I study how people experience, build, and sustain software—and how we can make those experiences more inclusive.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Oregon State University, advised by Dr. Margaret Burnett and Dr. Anita Sarma. My research sits at the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and Empirical Software Engineering.

My work explores software inclusivity, the impact of generative AI adoption on software development, developer productivity and burnout, and online community sustainability. Previously, I worked as a software engineer at Therap (BD) Ltd.

Research interests

  • Human–Computer Interaction
  • Empirical Software Engineering
  • Inclusivity & inclusive design
  • GenAI & developer well-being
